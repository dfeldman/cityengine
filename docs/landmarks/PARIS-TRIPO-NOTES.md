# Tripo candidates, as they turn up

What Tripo is for (owner, 2026-10-30): statues, sculptures with complex curves, finials and specific architectural details like complex domes, complex fountains, and boats seen from a distance. It can't do transparency, and it works poorly for anything building-sized you can get close to. About 100 models a month, so rank them. A procedural stand-in comes first regardless: distant views need one anyway.

For the owner to model later. Add a line: name · lat, lon · what it is · what a photograph needs to show · why procedural won't do. The full list with reference folders is PARIS-STATUES.md (tools/paris-statues.ts).
