## Kit adequacy (kit `word: temple` = towerless hipped hall, pediment, oculus)

Judged from recipes.ts and general knowledge, not shots:
- **Very bad → custom:** Église Américaine (Quai d'Orsay; tall Gothic-revival spire tower, kit gives none).
- **Off but secondary:** Temple de Pentemont (domed 18th-c. chapel), Temple de l'Étoile (big Romanesque arched front), Billettes (classical front with belfry, cloister), Christuskirche / Swedish / Danish (small Gothic gable fronts).
- **Fine or skip:** Rédemption, Batignolles, Saint-Esprit (set back behind street buildings), St Michael's & Scots Kirk (modern, inside buildings), the small parish temples.

**Owner, 2026-11-04:** the kit is generally insufficient — the "off but secondary" group gets custom models too.
Queue: Oratoire → St George's → Église Américaine → Étoile → Pentemont → Billettes → Christuskirche → Swedish → Danish → Rédemption → Batignolles → remaining visible ones by size.

**Owner, 2026-11-04:** the kit is generally insufficient. Model the large ones and any small one with a Wikipedia article; skip ones without good photos or not visible from the street.
