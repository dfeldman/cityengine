# Église Sainte-Rosalie (13e, OSM way/78407279)

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/sainte-rosalie.ts (entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json). Footprint from the local OSM way (43 nodes);
heights from IGN LiDAR MNS minus MNT (nave ridge ~16.5 m, eaves ~11 m, side annex ~9 m; a 26 m apartment block stands on the north side).
Photos (Commons, refs/sainte-rosalie/): Bd_Blanq.jpg (front block + crenellated stone gable), __glise_.jpg (flank with round windows).

Built: two-storey cream-rendered street block with arched doors and shuttered windows; stone gable over it with crenellated raking edge, pinnacles, rose and lozenge;
slate nave with round flank windows; lower side annex; plain east end.
Weaknesses: tightly walled by apartment blocks so hard to see from the street; apse and stepped side walls simplified; shutters and rear details not modelled; AXIS_DEG -55 is estimated from the footprint edges.
