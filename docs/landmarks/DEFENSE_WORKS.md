# La Défense works still to model

Every outdoor sculpture, installation and memorial on and around the dalle (box 2.220–2.260 E, 48.882–48.899 N) that the
game doesn't draw yet (owner, 2026-11-24: "check every monument/sculpture/statue/etc in La Défense … find any that are
not and do them"). Sources: OSM's artwork/memorial/fountain/statue features in that box (tools/cache/osm, osmium), checked
against every landmark entry by OSM ref, name and distance; plus sections E and F of PARIS-STATUES.md. Already drawn:
Calder's Araignée Rouge, César's Pouce, Agam's fountain, the Bassin Takis, Moretti's tower, Barrias's La Défense de Paris,
the Cheminée végétale, Les Trois arbres, Vive le Vent, Les Bancs et la Pyramide noire, Arc déplacé, the Temple de l'Amour
(Neuilly). Left out: murals, paintings and mosaics (flat), Patrick Blanc's green wall, Wallace fountains (drawn by the
street-furniture pass), plaques, the dozens of unnamed jet nodes and pools of the dalle's water features.

Method: docs/landmarks/MINOR-MONUMENT-BRIEF.md (procedural for the abstract and architectural parts, img2glb guided for
figures). Shots with CITY=paris-centre; x = (lon − 2.332)·73270, z = (lat − 48.8605)·111200.

## Groups

- **abstract-west** (procedural): Takis's Signaux Lumineux, Serra's Slat, Kowalski's L'Escalier, Buren's two installations,
  Miyawaki's Utsurohi, Philolaos's Les Cheminées, the CNIT sculpture, Bufalino's car on a lamp post.
- **abstract-east** (procedural): Lim Dong Lak's Point – Croissance, Morellet's La Défonce, Venet's sculpture, Atila's
  Sculpteur de nuages, Jankovic, Caro's After Olympia, Lancelin's Cube Sphère Gold, BC1, Bourgeat's Banc public, Les Nymphéas
  and L'Oiseau mécanique, Half Hare.
- **figurative-west** (guided figures on procedural bases): Mitoraj's Tindaro, Ikaria and Colosse, Emily Young's Quatre
  Têtes, Barelier's Le Téléphone, La Connaissance, the "Bronze" node, Fenosa's Le Beau Temps, Pirot's L'Envol, Rieti's Poire and
  Serpent.
- **figurative-east**: Mitoraj's Grand Toscano, Miró's Couple d'amoureux, Belmondo's stèle Pouvreau, Delfino's Lieux du corps,
  Caillard's Hercules, Silva's Dame Lune, Derbré's La Terre, Torricini's La Grenouille, Fenosa's Ophélie, Selinger's La Danse,
  de Miller's Le Somnambule, Les Hommes de la cité, Leygue's Fontaine des corolles, Busato's Fontaine du dialogue.
- **memorials-west**: Karavan's Perspective, Castelbajac's Totem, the Grande chaise rouge, the Marcel Proust installation, the
  Général de Gaulle and Résistance memorials (Puteaux) and de Gaulle (Courbevoie), the Mémorial Napoléon, Jaurès (Puteaux and
  Courbevoie), Perronet, the Lechevallier bust, the Oiseau de Jaz.
