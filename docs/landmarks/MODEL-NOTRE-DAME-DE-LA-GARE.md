# Église Notre-Dame-de-la-Gare (place Jeanne-d'Arc, 13e)

OSM way/79084116; Claude Naissant, 1847-59, neo-Romanesque. Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/notre-dame-de-la-gare.ts (`notreDameDeLaGare`); entry appended to public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json (front 318).

- Photo: Commons "NDG Biais.JPG" (facade and south flank) and the interior P1260723; refs in docs/landmarks/refs/notre-dame-de-la-gare/.
- Lidar (IGN MNS, grid.txt, 2026-11-04): nave ridge 22 m, aisle roofs 10-12 m, side chapels ~8 m, tower and spire 35 m. Nave axis runs -48 deg from east (facade at the north-west, apse south-east); footprint 23 m wide, 54 m long.
- Shape: nave 12 m wide, aisles 5.5 m each, 8 bays; half-octagon apse (r 6) inside a half-annulus ambulatory (r 11.2, 10.4 m); two stepped chapels each side between u -5 and 9; facade with round portal, rose, clock roundel, corner pilasters, steps; tower 6.5 m square with twin louvred arches and a slate pyramid spire with four dormers.
- Geometry fixes after comparing shots to the photo: the facade gable was drawn with the wrong axis name (floating triangle); the apse polygon had duplicate chord points and vanished; the tower was shortened and widened and the spire narrowed to match the photo's belfry-to-spire ratio.
- Weaknesses: spire is squatter than the photo's (no stone octagon transition, no lucarne gables); no coursing or the facade's pilaster strips; interior ignored; chapel roofs flat guesses; ambulatory ring is 0.8 m past the OSM apse.
