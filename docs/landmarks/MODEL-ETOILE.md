# Temple protestant de l'Étoile (way/84322974, wikidata Q15958175)

54 avenue de la Grande-Armée, Vaudremer 1874. Builder `templeEtoile` in src/render/landmarks/paris/protestant.ts; manifest entry in
public/landmarks/paris/protestant.json (the parisCivic entry in civic.json was removed). madeBy claude-sonnet-5-5.

- Refs: docs/landmarks/refs/etoile/a.jpg (portal), b.jpg (whole front), Commons "Temple Réformé Étoile - Paris XVII (FR75) - 2021-08-31 - 2/3". Geosearch found both at 25 m; the front is on the street, visible.
- The front is Gothic-revival, not Romanesque: pointed portal under a gablet, side doors under hoods, two roundels, stepped blind-arcade aisles, nave gable with tall pointed window, rose and cross.
- Frame: u along the avenue edge of the OSM ring (vertices 13 to 14, 19.6 m), v into the lot; registered with museums/enframe.ts. Everything on the front is photo pixels of b.jpg times ET_SX / ET_SY (constants at the top of the builder).
- Not modelled: the rear halls are one flat 9 m prism on the OSM plan; no side-wall windows (party walls); the nave roof is plain slate; carved foliage and the portal's angel brackets are omitted. Height scale is a guess (b.jpg is stretched upward): apex about 26 m.
