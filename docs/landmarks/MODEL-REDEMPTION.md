# Temple de la Rédemption (2026-11-04, claude-sonnet-5-5)

OSM way/69220900 (Lutheran, 16 rue Chauchat, Paris 9e, MH 1958; OSM ring 29 pts, ~37 x 28 m). Builder `templeRedemption` in
src/render/landmarks/paris/protestant.ts (RD_* constants), registered in procedural.ts, entry in public/landmarks/paris/protestant.json
(front 272 = west, the rue Chauchat side). The `parisCivic` entry for the same way was removed from civic.json.

Refs (Commons): refs/redemption/P1030037_Paris.jpg (front from the south-west, behind an iron railing: visible from the street),
Plan_du_Temple.jpg (plan, E-W nave about 2:1, apse at the east), Paris_9___Temp.jpg, P1220820_Paris.jpg. Shot: refs/redemption/shot-final.png
(in-city, lowres, bearing 268, HEIGHT 12; street-level cameras are walled by the flats).

Modelled: stone front block and OSM rear plan, cornice, slate gable roof, pediment with raking cornice, colossal voussoir arch, recessed
tympanum, two-column portico with inscribed entablature and segmental cap, round-headed red door, flank rustication courses, plinth and
iron railing, ridge lantern with pyramid cap and cross, round-headed windows on the long OSM walls.
Not verified / risks: all heights and widths are photo-scaled guesses (arch radius, eave 9 m, pediment 3.6 m; tune RD_*); the OSM front
is notched (recessed 3 m mid-span) and the model squares it off at the west extreme; side windows and the rear roof are guesses
(no photos of them); the lowres shot is too small to judge arch voussoir detail.
