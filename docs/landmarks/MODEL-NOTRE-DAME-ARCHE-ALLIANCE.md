# Notre-Dame de l'Arche d'Alliance (rue d'Alleray, 15e) - OSM way/24303512

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/notre-dame-arche-alliance.ts (`notreDameArcheAlliance`), entry in
public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json. Refs: docs/landmarks/refs/notre-dame-arche-alliance/ (Commons photos p2, p3;
IGN MNS grid/bands in lidar/; model shots in shots/).

- Footprint: OSM is an 18.5 m square turned -31.6 deg from east; the builder's frame has u along that axis, v across.
- Copper-brown cube (16.2 m, 19.5 m high) with small square windows; 2x2 glazing on the SW (street) face.
- Free-standing steel lattice frame on the OSM square, 4 bays a side, three ring beams, roof grid, 24 m.
- Grey stair block at the SE corner (15 m) with a curved lattice fin (27 m) and a round lattice bell tower
  (five bell discs, cross tip ~35 m). Tower tip from the LiDAR spike; trees blur the other heights.
- Tunables are at the top of the builder. Weaknesses: heights of cube vs frame are photo estimates; no fence, no
  neighbouring parish buildings, fin simplified to ribs; placement in the city not confirmed (a street shot was
  blocked by the Alleray block).
