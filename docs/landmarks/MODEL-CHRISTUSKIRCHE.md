Not modelled: the carved ornament detail, diagonal banding over the windows, the real attic bays.

Risks: the front is wedged between apartment blocks and the OSM outline is only ~12 m wide at the street; the storeys above the cornice (attic) are a guess and the nave interior/roof is invisible from the street. Shot: `refs/christuskirche/final.png` (lowres, bearing 30, HEIGHT 5).
