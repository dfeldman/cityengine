# Église Saint-Thomas-d'Aquin (2026-11-04, claude-sonnet-5-5)

Place Saint-Thomas-d'Aquin, 7e. OSM way/63536576 (wikidata Q3584573). Builder `saintThomasAquin` in
src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/saint-thomas-d-aquin.ts; entry in catholic.json (front 208.3 = outward normal, SSW).
Refs: docs/landmarks/refs/st-thomas-aquin/ (Commons photos way-63536576-0N.jpg, lidar/grid.txt + bands.png, ms/ model shots).

Measured (IGN MNS, church frame axis 61.7 deg): nave ridge ~28 m, aisles ~13 m, transept ~24 m (first pass), choir ~17 m,
front pediment apex ~28 m (photo scale 27 px/m). Front 28 m wide: six engaged columns below (entablature 12.9 m), four
paired above to 24.8 m, clock window, pediment, concave scroll wings with obelisks, cross to 31.5 m.
Geometry fix after comparing shot to photo: transept lowered to eave 18 / ridge 22.5 and trimmed so it does not tower over
the aisles (lidar's 24 m was the crossing, not the arms); scroll wings made stepped solids instead of flat decals.
Not modelled: relief panels, rusticated base, choir details, the neighbouring ministry buildings (lidar 25 m east of the choir).
In-city street shots are blocked by the narrow street; the facade was judged from model-shots --entry views.
