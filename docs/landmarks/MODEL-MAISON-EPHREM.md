# Maison d'accueil Ephrem (Montmartre, OSM way/67699838)

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/maison-ephrem.ts (entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json). Pilgrims' house of the Sacré-Cœur, east of the basilica.
Footprint from the local pbf (centroid 48.88684, 2.34366); heights from IGN LiDAR MNS minus MNT (main block ridge ~25 m, east wing 19-22 m, north block 25 m).
Photos (Commons, refs/maison-ephrem/): Maison_d_acc.jpg (gable + steep slate roof from the street), H__tellerie_.jpg (passage to the basilica). Shots: s.png (south), a.png (aerial).
Weaknesses: the arched passage/bridge to the basilica is not modelled; roofs are plain gables (the photo shows a hipped pyramid on the main block); dormers and windows are a regular grid; the ring was simplified by hand from 66 nodes.
Skipped rows: 143 Salésiens de Don Bosco and 179 Chapelle Ozanam (no Commons photo, geosearch and name search).
