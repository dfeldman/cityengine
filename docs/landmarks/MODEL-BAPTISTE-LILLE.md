# Église évangélique baptiste, 48 rue de Lille (Paris 7e)

- OSM way 63149138 (temple, 1873). Builder `baptisteLille` in `src/render/landmarks/paris/protestant.ts`; entry appended to `public/landmarks/paris/protestant.json`; the generic parisCivic entry for the same way was removed from `civic.json`.
- Refs (Commons, 2024-11-08 photos and P1250764): `docs/landmarks/refs/baptiste-lille/ref-*.jpg`. Front elevation measured in photo pixels (constants `BL_*`).
- Front faces SSW onto rue de Lille (south end of the OSM strip; the northern bits of the ring are annexes behind). Gothic-revival ashlar front: crocketed pointed portal, trefoil tympanum, 2 lancet triplets + 2 shopfronts, rose window between two traceried windows, cornice with balustrades and pinnacles, central gable with three louvred lancets. Nave is a prism on the OSM plan with a slate ridge.
- Shot: `shot-front.png` (CITY=paris-centre, target -207.6,-196.8, bearing 204, HEIGHT 5, PITCH 12, lowres).
- Not modelled: carved crockets/foliage, rose-window tracery detail, roof/annexe detail behind the front.
