# Temple protestant de Boulogne-Billancourt (way/68723367)

117 rue du Château. Builder `templeBoulogne` at the end of `src/render/landmarks/paris/protestant.ts`; manifest entry in `public/landmarks/paris/protestant.json` (front -15, NNW onto rue du Château). The generic parisCivic entry for the same way was removed from `civic.json`.

Refs (Commons, 2026-11-04): `docs/landmarks/refs/boulogne/ref-1..4.jpg`.

Read off the photos: cream limestone gable (10 m) with red brick pilaster strips, a polychrome brick arch over three stepped arched windows, a gabled stone hood over the oak door, a brick-arched slit and a stone bellcote with a cross; a low ochre pavilion with slate mansard and dormer to the west.

Plan caveat: the OSM ring covers the gable bay (10 x 5 m) and a big block to the west; the nave behind the gable is not in the ring (a courtyard in the cadastre), so the nave volume (`BO_NAVE_D` = 12 m) slightly overhangs the ring to the SE, against neighbour way/68723296.
All dimensions are `BO_*` constants. The rear block is a flat slate-topped box (not photographed).
