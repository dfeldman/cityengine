# Chapelle Saint-Sauveur / église maronite Saints-Sauveur-et-Maroun (Issy-les-Moulineaux, OSM way/42268952)

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/chapelle-saint-sauveur.ts (entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json). Cruciform chapel in the Corentin-Celton hospital garden.
Footprint from the local pbf (90 nodes; the way and its duplicate relation are one church); roof heights from IGN LiDAR MNS (nave ridge ~18.5 m, aisle walls ~8.5 m, transept ~18 m).
Photos (Commons "Chapelle St Sauveur Issy Moulineaux 1-4", refs/chapelle-saint-sauveur/): tower and front gable down the garden avenue; nave flank with arcade.
Weaknesses: the tower and spire are photo-estimated (MNS shows nothing above 19 m there; which end is the front is inferred from the flank photo); the low cloister arcade
beside the nave lies outside the OSM outline and is not modelled; window and portal detail generic; scaffolding in the photos ignored.
