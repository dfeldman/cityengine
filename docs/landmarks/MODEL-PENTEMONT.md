# Temple de Pentemont (way/67353449)

Former abbey church of Panthemont (Contant d'Ivry, 1756), 106 rue de Grenelle. Builder `templePentemont` in
`src/render/landmarks/paris/protestant.ts`; entry in `public/landmarks/paris/protestant.json`; the generic `parisCivic`
entry for the same way was removed from `civic.json`.

- Refs: `docs/landmarks/refs/pentemont/ref-a..d.jpg` (Commons: Abbaye de Penthemont 104-106 rue de Grenelle 002; Temple de Pentemont; Paris 7e Temple de Pentemont 571; Facade PH80555).
- Plan: OSM ring, origin (-742,-450) in the local frame; front = edge (3.8,-20)->(-17.4,-7.7), faces 210 deg (rue de Grenelle).
- Built: limestone main block, pediment, portal (two columns, hood, door), side doors with hoods and oculi, curved attic with glazed half-round window, slate dome on a 16-sided drum with oculi, lantern, vane, hooded barred windows on the long walls.
- Tunables: `PM_*` constants (heights, dome, portal, window pitch).
- Shot: `refs/pentemont/shot-a.png` (CITY=paris-centre, target -746,-458, bearing 200, dist 1.3, HEIGHT=30 PITCH=38).
- Risks: the front sits between ministry wings and is hard to photograph at street level; portal and pediment detail were not verified in a close shot. Attic and dome heights are estimated from photos.
