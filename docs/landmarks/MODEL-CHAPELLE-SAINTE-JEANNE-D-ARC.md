# Chapelle Sainte-Jeanne-d'Arc (14e, OSM way/118528607, Q2957104)

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/chapelle-sainte-jeanne-d-arc.ts (registered in procedural.ts; entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json).
Neo-Gothic chapel of buff stone, nave axis north-south, front (tower) to the south.

Measured (IGN MNS, refs/chapelle-sainte-jeanne-d-arc/grid.txt, bands.png): nave walls and parapet 13 m, steep slate gable ridge ~19 m, tower 4.3 m wide
with belfry to 24 m and spire tip ~35 m (lidar sees only 32 at the needle), half-hexagonal apse 13 m with cone to ~18 m, low chapel ring 4.6 m.
Photos (Commons, Paris 14e - Chapelle Sainte-Jeanne-d'Arc): f.jpg front, a.jpg apse, v.jpg general view.

Weaknesses: no transept (none seen); the low wing left of the front (sacristy) is omitted; parapet is a plain band, not pierced; spire has no lucarnes;
stone colour is a guess from the photos (weathered buff-grey, tuned darker after the first in-city shot); neighbours abut the nave sides.
