# Statues worth a real model (central Paris box)

User request, 2026-09-21: freestanding statues that sit roughly at a rider's eye level, or are very prominent;
busts fine; nothing built into a wall. Ranked by how much each would improve what a rider sees. Coordinates
are approximate (to ~20 m) — the loader places a model by the manifest's lat/lon and a turn, and the view tool
checks it.

**Already have GLBs (user):** the Génie de la Liberté (top of the Colonne de Juillet), the Cirque d'Hiver
horsemen (two).

## Worth it if models turn up

- Lafayette, equestrian (Cours la Reine, 48.8637, 2.3170) — freestanding in a garden by the quai.
- Churchill (Avenue Winston-Churchill by the Petit Palais, 48.8662, 2.3136).
- Washington and Lafayette (Place des États-Unis, 48.8667, 2.2939).
- Jeanne d'Arc, equestrian (Place Saint-Augustin, 48.8757, 2.3196) — at the box's north edge.

## Not recommended

Wall-mounted or niche sculpture (the Fontaine Saint-Michel's Saint Michael, the Louvre's facade figures, the
Opéra's groups against its front), the Fontaine des Innocents (architecture — better procedural), the obelisk
(geometry — procedural is better), and the Rodin garden (behind walls, not seen from the road).
