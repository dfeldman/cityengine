# Chapelle de Conflans (Charenton-le-Pont, OSM way/149268044, Q2957177)

Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/chapelle-de-conflans.ts (registered in procedural.ts; entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json).
Neo-Gothic chapel of buff limestone in the Conflans seminary park. Cross plan: nave NW-SE (axis -43 deg), transept with gabled portal front to the SW, half-decagon apse to the SE, low chapels flanking it.

Measured (IGN MNS, refs/chapelle-de-conflans/grid.txt, bands.png): nave eave ~15 m, ridge ~18.4 m, transept ridge ~17.4 m, apse peak ~18.5 m, low chapels ~11 m; NW end of the nave abuts a 23 m seminary block (not modelled).
Photos (Commons, "Chapelle Conflans - Charenton-le-Pont (FR94) - 2021-01-03" 1, 3, 5, 6, 7): photo1 nave lancets, photo3 portal, photo5 apse, photo7 SW front.
Shot: refs/.../low-front.png (CITY=paris-centre, 5377 -4347, inside the detail box; renders).

Weaknesses: transept eave (11.8 m) is a guess from photo 7, as the lidar only gives the ridge; the nave roof reads shallow because the lidar gives only 3 m of rise over 17 m; low-chapel and apse-flank outlines are rough; no pinnacles/gablets on buttresses, no tracery, no wing at the SW flank; the transept's NE arm is plain.
