# Église Sainte-Jeanne-de-Chantal (porte de Saint-Cloud, 16e)

OSM way/87959071 (church) + way/87959097 (independent belfry, tagged height 60, lidar says ~40 m incl. cross).
Builder: src/render/landmarks/paris/catholic/sainte-jeanne-de-chantal.ts (`sainteJeanneDeChantal`), entry in public/landmarks/paris/catholic.json.

Measured: OSM building:parts (front block gable 15 m, nave 15 m, transept 15 m, dome 25 m, chevet 8 m, chapels 8-10 m, baptistery pyramidal 8 m), IGN MNS grid (refs/.../grid.txt, 2.5 m cells, trees inflate flanks to 12-18 m).
