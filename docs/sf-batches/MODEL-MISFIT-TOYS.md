# Misfit Toys on the JFK Promenade: the LOVE Blocks and the jacks (2026-10-27)

Owner: "Love blocks at 244 JFK drive. Then giant jacks across the street which are newer", with two photographs and a
satellite view. Builders in `src/render/landmarks/sf/misfit-toys.ts`, entries in `public/landmarks/san-francisco/parks.json`.

## Sources

- **OSM** node/12641813205, "LOVE blocks, by Misfit Toys" — the blocks' lat/lon. The Garfield monument (node/1350891714)
  stands just north-east; the owner's night photograph has it behind the V block, which fixes the row's direction.
- **Rec & Park press release** (March 2026, via the Richmond Review): "Where's the Ball" by Misfit Toys, "10 large-scale
  sculptures made of galvanized steel that replicate jacks", "on the lawn along the Promenade at Nancy Pelosi Drive".
  No sizes given; scaled from the photographs (blocks 1.6–1.8 m, jacks about 1.4 m high).
- The jacks are not in OSM and postdate the satellite picture.

## What is drawn

- **Blocks**: four weathered-wood cubes (planks surface), each face framed and carrying a raised painted glyph from a small
  stroke font — L red, O slate, V green, E red, with A, C, 2, 4, 7, F round the sides. Each tipped (yaw, then about its
  own x and z) and sunk so its lowest corner is 0.5 m under the grass. ~1 800 triangles.
- **Jacks**: six arms on three axes with a ball on every end, lying as a thrown jack lies (the +x/+y/+z diagonal straight
  down, on three balls), yaws spread round. ~7 400 triangles for the ten.

## Placement

The jacks stand on the lawn between the Promenade and the trail south of it, each at least 3.2 m from every OSM way's
line (9.5 m from the Promenade's), 4 m apart. Two earlier spreads put jacks on the roadway and then two on the trail: the
check took only ways with a vertex near the lawn, and the trail's vertices are 30 m off at either end. Check against
every segment.
