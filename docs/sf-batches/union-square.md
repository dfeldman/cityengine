## What was done (2026-09-22)

Thirty buildings modelled one by one and the square itself; every other building fronting Geary, Post, Sutter,
O'Farrell, Powell, Stockton, Grant, Mason and Maiden Lane between Mason and Kearny given a facade style
(177 restyle entries). The builders are `src/render/landmarks/sf/districts/union-square.ts`; the entries are
`public/landmarks/san-francisco/union-square.json`; the shots are `docs/shots/models/union-square/`.
