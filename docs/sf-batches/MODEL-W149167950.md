## What it is

A 2002 nine-storey office building of the post-ballpark South Beach kind: **red-brown brick** walls in a regular
grid of large windows, a dark storefront base with shops/restaurants, iron balconettes on some floors, and on top a
**curved steel arch / barrel frame** spanning the roofline with a **round emblem/clock** at its centre — the thing
you see from King Street.
