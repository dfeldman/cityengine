# Generic buildings left on Market Street and in the FiDi core (2026-10-07)

The owner, 2026-10-07: "make a list of the generic buildings that are still on market street or in the fidi core AND are still generic (no custom model, maybe just a reface). Prioritize them for making custom models."

**What counts.** Every OSM building or part from the norcal extract that is
- on **Market Street**: its middle within 45 m of Market's centre line, from the Embarcadero to Van Ness; or
- in the **FiDi core**: north of Market, from Kearny (−122.4045) to the Embarcadero, up to Jackson (37.7972);

and that **no `procedural` manifest entry draws**: not claimed by id, its middle not inside a custom model's outline, and not inside a standalone model's box. A `restyle` (reface) still counts as generic and is marked. Left out: outlines under 60 m², underground or negative-layer outlines (the BART/Muni concourses), roofs and canopies, and parts lying inside an outline that is already listed.

**Totals:** 376 buildings — Market 87 (80 refaced), FiDi 289 (53 refaced).

**Priority score** = height × √footprint ÷ 10 (how much of the skyline and the street it is; 12 m assumed with no height tag), +60 named, +120 notable (wikidata/wikipedia/heritage/historic, marked ★), +40 on Market, +25 with no reface at all. It ranks what a rider sees most; check each against Mapillary before modelling (feedback: Street View first, recognisable not accurate), and grep the manifests and `ls` the builder path first (feedback-check-existing-landmark).

Generated by a one-off script over `tools/cache/osm/norcal-latest.osm.pbf` and `public/landmarks/san-francisco*.json`; rerun before trusting it after new models land.
